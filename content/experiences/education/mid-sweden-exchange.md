Exchange Semester in Sundsvall, Sweden. Applying systems concepts both on paper and on the ski slopes.
It was more project based, with real implementation of distributed systems and IoT. But also heavy on algorithm theory.

<!-- more -->

Five courses over the semester, graded on the Swedish scale from A (excellent) to F (fail). One hp equals one ECTS credit; the Swedish course is counted separately in fup (pre-education credits), so it comes on top of the 30 ECTS.

<!-- transcript table -->

The pattern says a lot about me: an A in the hands-on distributed systems course, and the grade dropping as the theory density increases, bottoming out at D for distributed algorithms (for my own defense, we were 4 students and everyone got terrible grades). The IoT course was my favorite content-wise — an HTTP/REST server, a CoAP client and an MQTT broker, all from scratch in Rust, no libraries allowed (see the [IoT project](https://github.com/NathanBrodin/IoT)), even if the transcript says D.

And Swedish: B. Jag kan lite svenska nu.
