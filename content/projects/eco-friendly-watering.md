For the 6-month project of my 3rd year of engineering school, I built a device to put on a plant that had sensors for temperature and humidity, running on an [STM32](https://www.st.com/en/microcontrollers-microprocessors/stm32-32-bit-arm-cortex-mcus.html) card. The card hosts a small web server written in C, and the [Flutter](https://flutter.dev/) app connects to it over Wi-Fi to fetch and display the data.

Made the sketches first on [Figma](https://www.figma.com/), with of course a nice UI, and then built it.
