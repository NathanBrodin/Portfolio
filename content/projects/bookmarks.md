A place where I save links to UI libraries, shadcn/ui registries, and websites that just look really good. When I need inspiration, I open a few of these and let my creativity do the rest. The UI is heavily inspired by [zed.dev](https://zed.dev). Built with [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/), [Drizzle](https://orm.drizzle.team/), and SQLite ([Turso](https://turso.tech/) (because their free tier is really generous)).
