## Weather App

For school we had to make a weather app in Flutter. I went all in with the UI, used Midjourney to generate the background images, so that it would show a nice landscape based on the weather. It fetches data from the OpenWeatherMap API: current conditions, temperature, wind speed, and a forecast for the next 5 days. Icons picked from a Dribbble shot, mockups made with shots.so. Since it was a school project, the app is in French only.
