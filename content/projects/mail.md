"Redefining Your Inbox Experience" is a mail client to rethink how you manage messages, prioritizing and responding with intuitive simplicity (that's what the README says at least).

[Next.js](https://nextjs.org/), [TypeScript](https://www.typescriptlang.org/), [Tailwind CSS](https://tailwindcss.com/) with [shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/), auth with [NextAuth.js](https://next-auth.js.org/) via GitHub, a discover page and a proper new-user experience, full i18n in English and French, theme customization, [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) for analytics, [Sentry](https://sentry.io/) for error tracking, and [Cypress](https://www.cypress.io/) E2E tests running in CI, with a stub login so the tests could get past the auth.
