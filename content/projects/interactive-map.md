## Interactive map

During my 4th year, made a full stack app for my software engineering class. https://gitlab.esiea.fr/brodin/interactive-map

The app lets you look for cities around you: you click on the map to search, change the search filters in a side panel, and click on a city to see more details about it.

All on Docker (one `docker compose up` and it runs), with CI/CD, docs with architecture diagrams, great doc. Client with NextJS, Tailwind, and Cypress for E2E testing. Backend with ASP .NET Core, C# and xUnit for unit tests, and Postgres db. OpenAPI specs, and great code coverage, with the pipeline and coverage badges directly in the README.
So this was a full stack app with all of the great software engineering practices.
